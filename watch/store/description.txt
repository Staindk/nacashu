Use your watch as a remote shutter for your phone's own camera app. Your camera app still takes the photo, so you keep all of its processing: HDR, Night Sight, portrait mode and the rest.

Android only. Needs the free Nacashu phone app: https://github.com/Staindk/nacashu

• SELECT takes a photo (press any button to stop a countdown or a run of photos)
• UP picks a timer: 0, 3, 5, 10 or 30 seconds
• DOWN picks how many photos to take: 1, 3, 5, 10 or ∞, about a second apart
• Hold UP to go back to no timer and one photo
• Hold DOWN to bring the camera to the front

The watch vibrates and shows whether each photo was taken. On Pebble Time 2 and Core 2 Duo, optional sounds tick down the timer and click when the photo is taken.

Set Nacashu as a Quick Launch app and turn on "Take photo when quick launching" in the phone app to take a photo from any watchface.

Works with Pixel Camera out of the box. For other camera apps, use "Set shutter button" in the phone app once: tap your camera's shutter and Nacashu remembers it.

The phone app has no internet permission, no accounts, ads or tracking. It's open source, so anyone can check what it does.
