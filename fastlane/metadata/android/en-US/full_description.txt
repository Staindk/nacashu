Nacashu turns your Pebble watch into a remote shutter for your phone's own camera app. Your camera app still takes the photo, so you keep all of its processing: HDR, Night Sight, portrait mode and the rest.

On the watch:
• SELECT takes a photo
• UP takes a photo after a timer (hold UP to change it: 1, 3, 5, 10 or 30 seconds)
• DOWN opens the camera
The watch vibrates and shows whether the photo was taken.

Set Nacashu as a Quick Launch app on the watch to take a photo from any watchface.

Works with Pixel Camera out of the box. For other camera apps, use "Set shutter button" once: tap your camera's shutter and Nacashu remembers it.

You need the Nacashu watchapp from the Pebble app store, and the Pebble app from Core Devices.

How it works
Nacashu uses Android's accessibility service to press the shutter button in your camera app, the same way a screen reader presses buttons. It reads your chosen camera app's screen only to find the shutter, and only when your watch asks. The app explains this and asks you to agree before it opens Android's accessibility settings.

Privacy
• No internet permission: Nacashu can't send anything off your phone.
• No accounts, ads or tracking.
• Settings stay on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall.

Open source
Nacashu is open source, so anyone can check what it does. Source code, issues and the privacy policy are on GitHub: https://github.com/Staindk/nacashu
